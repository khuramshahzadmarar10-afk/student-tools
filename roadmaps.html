MDCAT Preparation Roadmap

The MDCAT is a 200-MCQ entry test set by the Pakistan Medical & Dental Council (PMDC), drawn mostly from your FSc textbooks: Biology carries the largest share, then Chemistry and Physics, with smaller sections for English and Logical Reasoning. This free MDCAT preparation roadmap turns that syllabus into a realistic 24-week plan — about five to six focused study hours a day — whether you are preparing alongside FSc or after it. Work through the phases in order, practise MCQs from the very first month, and save the last weeks purely for past papers, mocks and revision.

Programming & Freelancing Roadmap (After FSc / Inter)

Finished FSc or intermediate and want an income skill? This free roadmap takes you from computer basics to your first freelancing client in about six months of steady daily work. You will learn one clear path — web development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript (Python is the alternative) — using only free courses, then turn that skill into practice projects, a portfolio, and profiles on Fiverr and Upwork. No paid course is required at any step; what it does require is consistency.
