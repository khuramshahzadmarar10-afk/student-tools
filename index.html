Student CV writing tips

Do

  • Lead with your strongest education, project, internship, or volunteer experience.
  • Use action verbs and add specific outcomes when you can verify them.
  • Tailor your objective and skills to each role or course application.
  • Proofread names, dates, grammar, email, and phone number before sending.

Don’t

  • Claim skills, grades, certificates, or experience you cannot support.
  • Use long paragraphs when concise bullet-style descriptions are clearer.
  • Add sensitive personal details unless the application specifically requires them.
  • Send the same generic CV without checking the job or program requirements.

Student Tool FAQs: Resume, CGPA, Merit, Pomodoro & Citations

How is CGPA calculated from semester GPA?

A simple CGPA averages all semester GPAs. A weighted CGPA is usually more accurate: multiply each semester GPA by its credit load, add those results, then divide by the total credits. Check your institution’s grading policy for its exact method.

Which merit formula should I use in Pakistan?

There is no single nationwide formula. Universities and programs set their own Matric, Intermediate, and entry test weights, and these can change by admission cycle. Enter the weights shown in the latest official prospectus.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The classic pattern is 25 minutes of focused work followed by a short break. After several sessions, take a longer break. The timer is a structure, not a rule—adapt the break length to your workload.

Are generated citations ready to submit?

They are a strong starting point, but citation rules can vary by source type, teacher, and edition. Review the author order, capitalization, publication date, italics, URL, and any course-specific requirements.

How do I make a strong student resume without work experience?

Lead with relevant education, course projects, volunteer work, internships, leadership, competitions, or part-time responsibilities. Describe what you did with clear action verbs and include specific, truthful outcomes where possible.

Which resume template should a student choose?

Modern works well for most applications, Classic suits formal academic or traditional roles, and Minimal keeps attention on concise content. Choose the style that fits the opportunity and keep the wording easy to scan.

How can I save the resume as a PDF?

Use the Download resume button to save a self-contained HTML copy. Open that file, choose Print from your browser menu, then select Save as PDF. Check the preview and page breaks before sending it.

External services

The page loads Google Analytics and web font files from Google-operated services. When your browser connects to those services, Google may receive technical request data such as your internet address, browser details, and request time under its own policies.
