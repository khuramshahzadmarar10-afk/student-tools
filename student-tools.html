Questions students ask

How is CGPA calculated from semester GPA?

A simple CGPA averages all semester GPAs. A weighted CGPA is usually more accurate: multiply each semester GPA by its credit load, add those results, then divide by the total credits. Check your institution’s grading policy for its exact method.

Which merit formula should I use in Pakistan?

There is no single nationwide formula. Universities and programs set their own Matric, Intermediate, and entry test weights, and these can change by admission cycle. Enter the weights shown in the latest official prospectus.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The classic pattern is 25 minutes of focused work followed by a short break. After several sessions, take a longer break. The timer is a structure, not a rule—adapt the break length to your workload.

Are generated citations ready to submit?

They are a strong starting point, but citation rules can vary by source type, teacher, and edition. Review the author order, capitalization, publication date, italics, URL, and any course-specific requirements.
